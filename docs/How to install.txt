SCREENSHOOTER
=============

Smart screenshots: point at something and the app finds the window, panel,
chat, message or page element by itself and highlights it. Click, and the
screenshot is ready. Screenshots are saved to the Desktop and land on a shelf
in the notch, from where you can drag them into any app.

INSTALLING
1. Drag the Screenshooter icon onto the Applications folder next to it.
2. Open the Applications folder in Finder and launch Screenshooter.
   The app has no main window: it lives in the menu bar (a viewfinder icon)
   and in the notch.

IF macOS WON'T OPEN THE APP
The app isn't notarized by Apple, so macOS may show a warning the first time.
You need to allow it only once.

  • On macOS 15 and later, close the warning with "Done", open System Settings
    → Privacy & Security, scroll down, click "Open Anyway" next to
    Screenshooter and confirm.

  • On macOS 14, right-click Screenshooter in Finder, choose "Open" and click
    "Open" again in the warning.

  • If that doesn't help, open Terminal and run:
        xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Screenshooter.app

FIRST LAUNCH
In the welcome window, allow:
  • "Screen Recording" — needed for any screenshot. macOS may ask to restart
    the app afterwards.
  • "Accessibility" — so the app recognizes buttons, messages, lists and page
    elements under the pointer.
  • access to the Desktop folder — screenshots are saved there.

TAKING SCREENSHOTS
  ⇧⌘2   smart capture: point at what you need and click
  ⇧⌘1   the whole screen at once
While capturing:
  ↑ ↓ or the scroll wheel   a larger or smaller area (message → chat →
                            window → screen)
  drag                      select an area by hand
  Space                     the whole window
  Esc                       cancel

THE SHELF IN THE NOTCH
Point at the notch at the top of the screen to open the shelf. Drag a
screenshot into a messenger or Finder, copy it, open it in the editor
(arrows, boxes, text, highlighter, pixelation, crop) or remove it. Drop an
image onto the notch to put it on the shelf.

Shortcuts, the screenshots folder and everything else can be changed in the
settings: the menu bar icon → "Settings…".
